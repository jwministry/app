Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and distributed by Elena Davydova across all platforms, including the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

1. Information Collection and Usage

Our priority is user privacy. By default, our applications operate under strict data minimization principles:

  • We do not collect, harvest, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, biometric data, or phone number.
  • No user accounts or sign-ups are required to use the essential features of our apps, unless explicitly specified within the interface.

2. Third-Party Platform Services

Depending on your operating system, our applications may utilize standard system-level services provided by platform operators to enable app functionality, updates, and reliability:

  • Apple Services: May process telemetry, in-app interactions, and system diagnostics as outlined in Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Supports core APIs and essential runtime dependencies. Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Crash Reports and Diagnostics

To help us maintain app stability and eliminate bugs, anonymized system diagnostic logs (such as device architecture, OS build version, and stack traces during unexpected app terminations) may be processed via Apple App Store Connect or Google Play Console. This data does not contain any personal information and cannot identify you personally.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our applications do not intentionally address or collect information from individuals under the age of 13. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with any data, please reach out via our contact email for immediate removal.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect technical or legal requirements. Any modifications become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
